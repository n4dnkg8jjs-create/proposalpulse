Privacy policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

The short version

  • We only read the page you're working on, and only to write your draft.
  • We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • Page content and drafts aren't stored on our servers. Your draft history stays in your browser.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

This policy explains what information the ProposalPulse Chrome extension and website ("ProposalPulse", "we", "us") collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. Contact us at csams0717@gmail.com with any questions.

What we collect

InformationWhy we need itWhere it's kept
Account details: your email address and basic Google profile (name, account ID) when you sign in with GoogleTo create your account, keep you signed in and link your subscriptionOur database (Supabase)

We never receive your card details. Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy. Uploaded files (PDF, Word, text) are converted to text on your own computer; the file itself is never uploaded.

When the extension reads web pages

  • Upwork and LinkedIn: the extension reads the job or profile you have open so it can show a match score and prepare a draft.
  • SAM.gov: it reads public contract opportunity details on the notice you have open.
  • Gmail and other websites: nothing is read unless you click Capture email or Scan page.
  • It does not read your browsing history, other tabs, passwords or form fields, and it never submits anything on your behalf.

Who we share data with

We share data only with the service providers that run ProposalPulse, and only what each needs:

  • Supabase (database, sign-in and server hosting, United States).
  • OpenAI (generates the draft text). We send the page content, your portfolio and your instructions for that request. Under OpenAI's API terms, this data isn't used to train their models by default.
  • Google (Sign in with Google).
  • Lemon Squeezy (payments, taxes and invoices, as merchant of record).

We do not sell personal information, share it for advertising, or use it to decide anyone's creditworthiness or eligibility for anything.

Chrome Web Store and Google user data

ProposalPulse's use of information received from Google APIs, and of data collected by the Chrome extension, adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy and the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use this data only to provide and improve ProposalPulse's single purpose: helping you write proposals and outreach.

How long we keep it

Account, portfolio backup, subscription and usage records are kept while your account is active. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete this data within 30 days, except where we must keep limited records for legal, tax or fraud-prevention reasons. Data stored in your browser is removed when you uninstall the extension.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Database access rules ensure each signed-in user can only read their own records, and sign-in tokens are kept inside the extension's background process rather than on web pages.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit or clear your portfolio at any time in the extension.
  • Delete draft history in the extension's History tab.
  • Email csams0717@gmail.com to access, correct, export or delete your data. We'll respond within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK), you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor them.

Children

ProposalPulse is a business tool and isn't intended for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we make material changes, we'll update this page and the date above, and tell signed-in users in the extension or by email.
