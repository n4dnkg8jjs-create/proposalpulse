Refund policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

The short version

  • Not happy with your first payment? Ask within 14 days for a full refund.
  • Cancel anytime; you keep access until the end of the month you paid for.

14-day money-back guarantee

If you're not satisfied after your first paid month on Solo or Pro, email us within 14 days of that first payment and we'll refund it in full. No long explanation needed.

Renewals

Monthly renewals aren't normally refunded, but you can cancel at any time to stop the next one. If you were charged for a renewal you didn't intend, contact us within 7 days and we'll sort it out.

Cancelling

Cancel from the link in your order or subscription email from Lemon Squeezy, or email us and we'll cancel for you. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period, then moves to the Free plan. You won't lose your portfolio or draft history.

How to request a refund

Email csams0717@gmail.com from the address you used to subscribe. Refunds are issued by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record, to your original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days depending on your bank.

This policy doesn't affect any rights you have under the consumer laws where you live.
